Project Introduction

Impact of social media on life

Social media is a massive part of our daily lives, shaping how we communicate, learn, and relax. As active users ourselves, we find this topic incredibly relevant. However, the rise in screen time has sparked important questions about student mental health and stress. Since everyone's online habits and stress levels are different, we wanted to look closer at how these two connect.

For this project, we decided to investigate a specific question:

Which of three social media habits — daily usage duration, late-night usage, or frequency of social comparison — is most strongly associated with perceived stress among students?

Stress is a major challenge for students, and social media plays a big role in it. By analyzing real-world data and designing an algorithm to process it, we want to figure out which of these online habits has the tightest link to high stress levels.
