Project Introduction

Impact of social media on life

This project is all about social media usage and its effect on female students. The topic of social media usage is very interesting to us because we are all social media users as well. Social media is a part of everyday life, and different platforms may attract different groups of female students. Their usage habits and stress levels may differ from one person to another. We find it interesting to investigate how much time students spend on social media and whether this is associated with their stress levels. The team shares a curiosity about how social media usage changes between weekdays and weekends, and whether students spend more or less time on these platforms during the weekends. In this project, we aim to investigate the number of hours students spend on social media each day, whether they use it late at night, and how using hours affect their stress score. By doing this project, we are trying to find a clue as to which habit has the strongest association with stress.
