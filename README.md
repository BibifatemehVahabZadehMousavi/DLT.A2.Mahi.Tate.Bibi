What problem do we want to solve?
1. Which social media platform is associated with the highest stress levels among female students, and how many hours do users spend on that platform on weekdays and weekends? (Tatevik)
   
  I am proposing this problem because it is well suited for algorithmic analysis. It combines different aspects of social media usage and stress into one research question. The problem requires several variables and a relatively large dataset, which makes the analysis more challenging and interesting. It also addresses a topic that is relevant to many students and may help us better understand the relationship between social media use and stress. The algorithm should identify the social media platform with the highest average stress level among female students. It should also calculate the average number of hours users spend on that platform during weekdays and weekends.

  
The variables that we can use: Gender- is needed to filter the dataset and analyze only female students.
Primary_Platform- is needed to identify which social media platform each student uses most often.
Perceived_Stress_Score- is needed to measure and compare stress levels across different platforms.
Daily_Usage_Hours- is needed to determine how much time students spend on social media during weekdays.
Weekend_Extra_Hours- is needed to determine how much time students spend on social media during weekends. (Leila)


The topic of social media usage is very interesting to me because I am also a social media user. Social media is a part of everyday life, and different platforms may attract different groups of female students. Their usage habits and stress levels may differ from one platform to another. I find it interesting to investigate how much time students spend on each platform and whether this is associated with their stress levels. I am also curious about how social media usage changes between weekdays and weekends, and whether students spend more or less time on these platforms during the weekends.

I think this topic is important because students' mental health may be related to their social media habits, which could also affect their academic success. By analysing this data, we may better understand the relationship between social media usage and stress among female students.

This topic is suitable for algorithmic analysis because it involves several variables and requires comparing information from different students. An algorithm can group students according to their primary social media platform, calculate the average stress level for each platform, and identify which platform is associated with the highest average stress level. It can also calculate the average number of hours students spend on that platform during weekdays and weekends. This will help us identify patterns in the data and develop a better understanding of the relationship between social media usage and stress levels. (Mahitab)
