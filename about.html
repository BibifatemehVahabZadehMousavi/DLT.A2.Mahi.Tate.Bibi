About our group

Meet the Team

We are three students working together on a project about social media and its relationship with stress among students. Here you can learn a little about us and the work we did.

Who We Are

Our team consists of Bibifatimeh, Tatevik, and Mahitab. We come from different educational and professional backgrounds, and each of us brought different skills and ideas to the project.

Our aim was to improve our logical thinking and data management skills by working with a dataset and developing an algorithm. We wanted to investigate how daily social media use, late night use, and social comparison relate to perceived stress among students.

How We Worked Together

Although we each had our main responsibilities, we did not work completely separately. We shared our progress, discussed problems, and asked each other for help when something was unclear. We also exchanged ideas before making important decisions.

Working together helped us understand different parts of the project, including preparing the data, planning the algorithm, and organizing our work. We also learned how important it is to communicate clearly and keep everyone updated.

This project was a learning experience for all of us. We had different tasks to complete, but we worked towards the same goal and helped each other along the way.
